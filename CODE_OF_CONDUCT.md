# Code of Conduct

We want WebSnap to be a friendly place to ask questions, share ideas and work together, whatever your experience,
background or identity.

## What we expect

- Be respectful and assume good faith. Disagree with ideas, not people.
- Give feedback that is specific and kind, and take feedback the same way.
- Welcome newcomers and answer questions patiently.
- Keep discussion on topic, and respect other people's time and privacy.

## What isn't acceptable

- Harassment, insults, threats or demeaning comments, including about someone's identity.
- Sexual or violent language or imagery, and unwelcome attention.
- Publishing other people's private information.
- Spam, or trying to use the project's issues or sharing features to attack others.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, issues and contributions that break these rules, and may restrict
participation by anyone who does. To report a problem, contact the maintainers through a private channel on the
repository's GitHub page (for example, a private message to the owner) rather than a public issue. Reports are
handled privately and fairly.

This applies in all project spaces and when someone is representing the project in public.
